This Privacy Policy explains how Helvorxy ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use CV Maker: Professional Resume (the "App").

The current App is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data through systems operated by Helvorxy. This description is intended to match the App Store declaration that data is not collected by the App.

1. Resume and Cover-Letter Content

You may enter names, contact details, employment history, education, skills, certifications, photographs, signatures, and other information to create a resume or cover letter. This content is provided voluntarily and is processed and stored locally on your device to create, preview, export, print, or share your documents.

Helvorxy does not receive or store your resume profile or document content on its own servers. You are responsible for ensuring that you have permission to use personal information or images belonging to another person.

2. Photos and Device Permissions

If you select a photograph or use another device feature, access is used only to perform the action you request. Permission is controlled through your device settings and may be changed or revoked there. Denying permission may prevent the related feature from working but does not prevent use of unrelated features.

3. Exporting, Printing, and Sharing

When you choose to export, print, or share a document through another application, printer, cloud provider, messaging service, or operating-system share sheet, that destination may process the content under its own privacy policy. Helvorxy does not control information after you choose to send it to an independent third party.

4. Analytics, Advertising, Accounts, and Tracking

The current App does not require an account and is not intended to collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, payment details, or cross-app tracking data through systems operated by Helvorxy. It does not sell or rent personal information.

Apple and your device or operating-system provider may independently process technical, diagnostic, distribution, or store information under their own policies. That platform-level processing is not controlled by Helvorxy.

5. Local Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Locally stored documents and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear App data where supported, reset the device, or uninstall the App. Device or cloud backups configured by you may retain copies under the backup provider's settings.

Because Helvorxy does not maintain a server-side resume database or user account for the current App, we ordinarily cannot retrieve, restore, export, or delete locally stored documents on your behalf.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise minors who enter or share personal information in a document.

7. Security

Keeping document content on the device reduces server-side exposure, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with appropriate access controls, install security updates, and maintain backups of important documents.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You control locally stored content through the App and device. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights. Because we do not ordinarily possess identifiable App data, we may have no server-side information to access, correct, or delete. If you believe Helvorxy controls information relating to you, submit a request through our contact page.

9. Third-Party Protection

If a future version uses a provider to process personal information on our behalf, that provider will be required to handle it consistently with this Policy, its contractual obligations, and applicable law. The Policy and relevant App Store disclosures will be updated before or in connection with a material change in data practices.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy when the App, technologies, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit https://helvorxy.com/contact.

This Privacy Policy explains how Helvorxy ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use GamePad Tester & Massager (the "App").

The App Store privacy disclosure identifies purchases and identifiers as data that may be linked to you for app functionality and analytics.

We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details. Apple processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Usage and technical information

The App may process technical and usage information such as IP address, device and operating-system version, app version, screens or features used, session timing, crash information, and performance diagnostics. This information is used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

Controller input

Button, trigger, stick, and vibration controls are processed to provide controller testing and massage functionality. We do not use controller input to identify you.

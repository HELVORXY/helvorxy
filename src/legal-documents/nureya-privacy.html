Privacy Policy for Nureya: Quran & Azan

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Nureya: Quran & Azan ("Nureya", "we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Nureya mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Nureya is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

The App may process certain information required to provide its core features, including:

Location Information

Nureya may request access to your device location in order to calculate accurate prayer times, Qibla direction, and other location-based Islamic features.

Your location information is used only for providing these features.

Where technically possible, location information is processed locally on your device and is not stored or sold by us.

You may deny or disable location access at any time through your device settings. Some features may not function correctly without location access.

Notification Permission

The App may request permission to send notifications for prayer times, Azan reminders, Islamic reminders, or other features selected by the user.

Notification settings can be changed or disabled at any time from your device settings.

Device and App Data

Your device and operating system may use information such as device type, operating-system version, application version, language, and local diagnostics to operate the App. The current App is not intended to transmit that information to systems operated by Helvorxy, consistent with the App Store declaration that data is not collected.

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts, or another service that materially changes data handling is introduced, this Policy and the relevant App Store disclosures will be updated before or in connection with that change.

2. Quran and Religious Content

Nureya may provide Quran verses, translations, prayer times, Azan reminders, Qibla information, Hadith content, Dhikr, or other Islamic resources.

User interaction with this content is generally processed locally and is not associated with a personal identity unless explicitly stated otherwise.

3. Personal Accounts

Nureya does not require users to create an account unless an account-related feature is introduced in a future version.

If account functionality is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Nureya does not sell users' personal information.

Unless explicitly disclosed inside the App or in an updated version of this Privacy Policy, Nureya does not use personal information for cross-app advertising or tracking.

5. Third-Party Services

Certain App features may rely on third-party services, APIs, operating-system services, or external content providers.

These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

If a provider processes personal information on our behalf in a future version, it will be required to handle that information consistently with this Policy, its contractual obligations, and applicable law.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or when necessary to protect the security, rights, and integrity of the App and its users.

7. Data Retention

Information stored locally by the App may remain on your device until you delete the information, reset the App, or uninstall the App.

If Nureya begins storing information on external servers in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated with relevant retention information.

8. Children's Privacy

Nureya is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in violation of applicable law, reasonable steps will be taken to remove it.

9. Data Security
